\textbf{Read this before you use LiNC on a machine.}

LiNC controls CNC machines and other potentially dangerous equipment. By using it you
acknowledge and agree to the following.

\begin{safetybox}
  A CNC machine moves rapidly and with great force. It can crush and trap, it throws broken
  tools and workpiece fragments, a spindle can start a fire, its electrical installation is
  hazardous, and cutting produces noise and dust. Do not operate CNC equipment unless you
  are trained and qualified to do so.
\end{safetybox}

\section*{Hardware, not software}

The emergency stop, the guards, the door interlocks, the limit switches and the machine's
own safety circuits are hardware. They must stay functional and independent of this
software, and they must never be bypassed. LiNC cannot see everything the machine does,
cannot prevent every programming error, and its interlocks (including the network
interlock) are convenience features, not safety functions.

the version of the notice, in the profile's preferences file
